Data: U.S. Census Bureau, LEHD Origin-Destination Employment Statistics (LODES8 v8.3), All Jobs (JT00), 2012, 2019, 2022.
Counts jobs, not individual workers. Self-employed and most agricultural workers are undercounted. Flows between Oregon and Washington counties come from the LODES "aux" files, which hold jobs whose holder lives in a different state from the workplace. Same-state flows come from the "main" files.
Correction: An earlier version of this tool showed flows between Oregon and Washington Gorge counties as zero and said the federal data withheld them. That was wrong. Those flows are published and are now shown. In a full recheck against the raw files, 74 of the tool's 90 figures changed.
"Flow A→B" = workers living in county A commuting to jobs in county B in that year.
